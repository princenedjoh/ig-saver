**Name:** IG Saver (the store disallows "Instagram" in extension names)
**Category:** Productivity
**Summary (132 chars max):** Adds a download button to posts, reels and stories on instagram.com. Photos save as JPG, videos as MP4.

**Description**
IG Saver adds a small download button to the media you're viewing on instagram.com.

- Posts, reels and stories, in the feed, on Explore and in pop-ups
- Carousels: saves the slide you're on
- Photos as JPG, videos as MP4, at full resolution
- Goes straight to your Downloads folder
- No account, no tracking, no data leaves your browser

For saving your own content or media you have permission to keep. Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

**Single purpose:** Save the photo or video the user is viewing on instagram.com to their computer.

**Permission justifications**
- `downloads`: saves the chosen photo or video file to the user's Downloads folder.
- Site access (`https://www.instagram.com/*`): injects the download button into the page and requests the media for the post the user clicked.
